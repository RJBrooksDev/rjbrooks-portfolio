Hailsi
in development · v1 ~3 mo out

Real-time voice translation overlay. Speak your language; they hear theirs.

A desktop app that captures audio (mic or system loopback), translates in real time, and overlays the result on whatever app you're in. Started as a personal Finnish ↔ English tool, growing into a multi-language productized version with a free tier and managed-API paid tiers. Privacy-first by default — no audio leaves the machine unless you want it to, regardless of which plan you're on.
